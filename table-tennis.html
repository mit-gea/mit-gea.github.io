BEFORE YOU PICK UP A PADDLE

The details.

When do matches need to be completed?

All preliminary matches must be completed and results submitted by Wednesday, November 18, 2026, ahead of the tournament on Friday, November 20.

Where do I find my partner or opponent?

The pairings and matchups sheet is the source of truth for your assignments. Check it before scheduling your match.

Where and when is the tournament?

Tournament day is Friday, November 20, 2026. The time and venue have not yet been announced here. Contact the social chairs for details.

Have a scheduling issue or a question about the format?

Email gea-officers@mit.edu. The social chairs can help with pairings, scheduling conflicts, and tournament rules.
